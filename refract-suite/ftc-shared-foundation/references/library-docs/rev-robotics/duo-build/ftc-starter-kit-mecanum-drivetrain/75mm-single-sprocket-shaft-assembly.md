# 75mm Single Sprocket Shaft Assembly

### Single Sprocket Shaft Assembly Parts Required

| **PART NUMBER**                                         | **DESCRIPTION**              | **QTY.** |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------- |
| [REV-41-1323](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1323/) | 15MM SPACER                  | 1        |
| [REV-41-1324](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1324/) | 3MM SPACER                   | 2        |
| [REV-41-1326](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1326/) | THROUGH BORE BEARING - SHORT | 2        |
| [REV-41-1327](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1327/) | SHAFT COLLAR                 | 1        |
| [REV-41-1338](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1338/) | 10 TOOTH #25 SPROCKET\*      | 1        |
| [REV-41-1347](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-41-1347/) | 5MM X 75MM HEX SHAFT         | 1        |

### Single Sprocket Shaft Assembly Steps

|                                                                     |                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <img src="/files/-MEOrUICSpi9XsMMsAl-" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Add a Shaft Collar flush to the end of the 5mm x 75mm Hex Shaft.</p><p><br>Tighten the Shaft Collar.</p>                                                               |
| <img src="/files/-MEOrsH303do75vLguAY" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Slide a Through Bore Bearing – Short onto the shaft.</p><p></p><p><strong>Note:</strong> the smaller portion of the bearing faces towards the Shaft Collar.</p>        |
| <img src="/files/-MEOsGskEPGLV6eHIp0I" alt="" data-size="original"> | Slide a 3mm Spacer onto the shaft.                                                                                                                                        |
| <img src="/files/-MEOqw7ItWTrHIDwEXzK" alt="" data-size="original"> | Slide a 15mm Spacer onto the shaft.                                                                                                                                       |
| <img src="/files/-MEOsbwXRWeXt3RnfQXU" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Slide a 10 Tooth # 25 Sprocket onto the shaft.</p><p></p><p>Note: For the Mecanum Drivetrain Kit, replace the sprocket with a 24 Tooth Pulley</p>                      |
| <img src="/files/-MEOt1U_Gu_JxLCd3Aif" alt="" data-size="original"> | Slide a 3mm Spacer onto the shaft.                                                                                                                                        |
| <img src="/files/-MEOtr2rQdo5nVY_jvTY" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Slide a Through Bore Bearing – Short onto the shaft.</p><p></p><p><strong>Note:</strong> the smaller portion of the bearing faces away from the Shaft Collar.</p>      |
| <img src="/files/-MEOu6wDqQnsMK9Pq3kw" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p><strong>SINGLE SPROCKET ASSEMBLY COMPLETE</strong><br></p><p>Repeat the above steps until there are two completed Single Sprocket Assemblies. Set aside for later.</p> |
