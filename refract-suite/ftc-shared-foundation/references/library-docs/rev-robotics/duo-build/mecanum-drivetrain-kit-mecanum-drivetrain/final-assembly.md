### Final Assembly Steps&#x20;

|                                                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <img src="/files/-MMMtTc2c2lcKcPosPlx" alt="" data-size="original"> | Slide the four Pre-Loaded brackets onto the Drive Rail Assembly as shown.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| <img src="/files/-MMN0HgfdTC2PENRwBHS" alt="" data-size="original"> | Align the Pre-Loaded Brackets where 6 Hex Cap Screws and Nylock Nuts are exposed.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| <img src="/files/-MMN0ruLX6KLvPG0V6SC" alt="" data-size="original"> | Add two Pre-Loaded Brackets to the Motor side C Channel.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| <img src="/files/-MMN5HBpicRB_TTzSGvb" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Use landmarks like the UltraPlanetary Output Bracket of the 9mm Bearing Seat on the Channel to align both 90 Degree Brackets to the same position on the Channel. </p><p></p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug</p>                                      |
| <img src="/files/-MMN7sScmfbnskyXpEUK" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Slide the 45mm X 15m C Channel - 248MM onto the Preloaded Brackets as shown.</p><p></p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                                                                                                           |
| <img src="/files/-MMMtTc2c2lcKcPosPlx" alt="" data-size="original"> | Repeat sliding four Pre-Loaded brackets onto the other Drive Rail Assembly as shown.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| <img src="/files/-MMN0HgfdTC2PENRwBHS" alt="" data-size="original"> | Repeat aligning the Pre-Loaded Brackets where 6 Hex Cap Screws and Nylock Nuts are exposed.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| <img src="/files/-MMN0ruLX6KLvPG0V6SC" alt="" data-size="original"> | Repeat adding Pre-Loaded Brackets to the Motor side C Channel.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| <img src="/files/-MMN5HBpicRB_TTzSGvb" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Use the same landmark from a few steps ago to align both 90 Degree Brackets to the same position on the Channel as the 90 Degree Brackets on the other Drive Rail. </p><p></p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug</p>                                     |
| <img src="/files/-MMN98UgfRkJ2wy6UoBp" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Slide the second Drive Rail on.</p><p></p><p>Once in place, tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                                                                                                                                         |
| <img src="/files/-MMNAJ5lv3MiHzxR-YjO" alt="" data-size="original"> | Slide two pieces of 15mm Extrusion – 420mm into the Pre-Loaded Brackets until they are through both sets brackets. One 15mm Extrusion is for the top and the other for the bottom.                                                                                     |
| <img src="/files/-MMNBuapOzWx6-9-PWFt" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Align the 15mm Extrusion – 420mm so approximately the same amount of Extrusion is left on the end of each Bracket.</p><p></p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                                                                     |
| <img src="/files/-MMNETMygW2OqkCpOFTi" alt="" data-size="original"> | Add a 15mm Spacer followed by a 3mm Spacer to the 90mm Hex Shafts that act as the Drive Shafts.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| <img src="/files/-MGA8uNn-f5RKA5S3lco" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Take the Mecanum wheels and place on the shafts as shown.                  </p><p></p><p><strong>Note:</strong> See the steps below to see how to place the left and right Mecanum Wheels. </p>                                                                     |
| <img src="/files/-MEdOvGHyh9ZFSpewGH_" alt="" data-size="original"> | Each Mecanum Wheel Set comes with two right (REV-41-1656) and two left (REV-41-1657) mecanum wheels. This is determined by the direction of the leading edge of the rollers. If the rollers point left it is a left wheel and if they point right it is a right wheel. |
| <img src="/files/-MGEhbw8k2D2JwY4u7qV" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Each side of the chassis needs one left and one right wheel. Place the wheels as shown. </p><p></p><p>See the<a href="/pages/-MFqon_8YHJhcaRVTT9e"> Mecanum Wheel Setup and Behavior </a>Section for more information. </p>                                         |
| <img src="/files/-MMNRg81qCM2OaL6b-3B" alt="" data-size="original"> | <p>Add one shaft collar to the end of each drive shaft. </p><p></p><p>Tighten down the shaft collars on each wheel to secure them in place.</p>                                                                                                                        |
| <img src="/files/-MMNSsSbB75Ym0K_W_Sb" alt="" data-size="original"> | **DRIVETRAIN COMPLETE**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
