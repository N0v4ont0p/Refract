| FINAL ASSEMBLY STEPS             |                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| ![](/files/-Me5tiprN4F2pjbXaP1T) | Add two Pre-Loaded Brackets to the Motor side C Channel.                                                                                                                                                     |

| ![](/files/-Me6-CIbYpmL-Yop82Zt) | <p>Slide the 45mm X 15m C Channel - 248MM onto the Preloaded Brackets as shown.</p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                                                        |
| ![](/files/-Me5ohT8Jlpv2owY3imL) | Repeat sliding four Pre-Loaded brackets onto the other Drive Rail Assembly as shown.                                                                                                                         |
| ![](/files/-Me5sTdJDEgXLHeu7tNr) | Repeat aligning the Pre-Loaded Brackets where 6 Hex Cap Screws and Nylock Nuts are exposed.                                                                                                                  |
| ![](/files/-Me5tiprN4F2pjbXaP1T) | Repeat adding Pre-Loaded Brackets to the Motor side C Channel.                                                                                                                                               |
| ![](/files/-Me5uaYjbS5oW24oyCee) | <p>Have the edge of the bracket roughly at the center of the large (9mm) hole. Start with the second hole to the right of the Double Sprocket Assembly.</p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug</p> |
| ![](/files/-Me6078FDutkwg0Tns--) | <p>Slide the second Drive Rail on.</p><p>Once in place, tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                                                                                      |
| ![](/files/-Me62JkJsd93pAQbBf60) | Slide two pieces of 15mm Extrusion – 420mm into the Pre-Loaded Brackets until they are through both sets brackets. One 15mm Extrusion is for the top and the other for the bottom.                           |
| ![](/files/-Me6IlHATyZZuHUWJJoD) | <p>Align the 15mm Extrusion – 420mm so approximately the same amount of Extrusion is left on the end of each Bracket.</p><p>Tighten the Nyloc Nuts until they are snug.</p>                                  |
| ![](/files/-Me6LoCxnPIFy7k3MgpU) | <p>Add a Through Bore Bearing – Short to both of the Drive Shafts and Through Bore Bearing – Long to the remaining shafts.</p><p>Note: the smaller section on the bushing faces away from the Channel</p>    |
| ![](/files/-Me6N2NMaIIAEEX5lt-C) | Take the Grip and Omni wheels and place on the shafts as shown.                                                                                                                                              |
| ![](/files/-Me6UXJbM1lfNs6qPC71) | Add one shaft collar to the end of each shaft.                                                                                                                                                               |
| ![](/files/-Me6U9frEMDNErujXQ6i) | Tighten down the shaft collars on each wheel to secure them in place.                                                                                                                                        |
