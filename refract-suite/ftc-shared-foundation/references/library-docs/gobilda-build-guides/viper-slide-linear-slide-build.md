# Viper-Slide Kit (Belt-Driven) — Assembly Guide

Covers both the **4-Stage** (SKU: 3210-0003-0004) and **2-Stage** (SKU: 3210-0003-0002) belt-driven Viper-Slide kits, 336mm slide length. Both kits use the identical assembly technique — the 4-stage kit simply repeats the "Middle Stage Subassembly" step to add extra extension. Differences between the two kits are called out inline. Motor is not included in either kit (a 5203 Series Yellow Jacket Planetary Gear Motor is shown as the reference example).

## Step 1 — Disassemble the Viper Slides

Slide the "Inner Piece" out of each "Outer Piece" (move against the direction of the printed arrow — there is resistance, but it separates with firm, steady force). Do this for all Viper Slides in the kit (four for the 4-stage kit, two for the 2-stage kit).

## Step 2 — Bottom Stage Subassembly

Fasten one Outer Piece to the Low-Side U-Channel (15-hole for the 4-stage kit, 13-hole for the 2-stage kit) using six 10mm Button Head Screws and six locknuts. Slide the Ball Carriage to access the middle holes. The "End-Stop Tab" should be on the left-hand side when mounted to the channel.

## Step 3 — Middle Stage Subassembly

Fasten one Outer Piece and one Inner Piece to two Pulley Brackets and one End-Stop Plate using nine 5mm Button Head Screws. The End-Stop Tab on the Outer Piece must align with the arrow on the Inner Piece.

- **4-stage kit:** repeat this step three times, for three identical Middle Stage Subassemblies.
- **2-stage kit:** build only one Middle Stage Subassembly.

## Step 4 — Top Stage Subassembly

Attach two Pulley Brackets and one End-Stop Plate to the last remaining Inner Piece using six 4mm Button Head Screws.

## Steps 5–6 — Stack the stages

Slide a Middle Stage Subassembly onto the Bottom Stage Subassembly, extending it fully so the Ball Carriage aligns with the slide's working range.

- **4-stage kit:** repeat to stack all three Middle Stage Subassemblies, then add the Top Stage Subassembly on top using the same procedure.
- **2-stage kit:** add the Top Stage Subassembly directly onto the (single) Middle Stage Subassembly.

## Steps 7–10 — Motor-side mounting bracket

7. Attach the Idler Arm to a Quad Block Mount using two 8mm Socket Head Screws (2-stage kit: one 8mm Button Head Screw + one 8mm Socket Head Screw).
8. Attach that subassembly to a 1-Hole U-Channel using three 12mm Socket Head Screws (2-stage kit also involves a Dual Block Mount here, fastened with one 12mm Socket Head Screw, one 12mm Button Head Screw, and two locknuts).
9. Fasten this subassembly to the Low-Side U-Channel using two 8mm Socket Head Screws (2-stage kit: fasten the Step 6 stack + a second Dual Block Mount together with five 12mm Socket Head Screws — only three of which thread into the Quad Block Mount).
10. Attach a 90° Angle Bracket between the Low-Side U-Channel and the 1-Hole U-Channel using two 12mm Socket Head Screws and two locknuts. (2-stage kit: this is Step 11; its Step 10 instead fastens the stacked-stage assembly with one 12mm Socket Head Screw, confirming the Pulley Brackets face up and the slide extends correctly — see Figure 10-A in source.)

## Steps 11–13 (4-stage) / 12–14 (2-stage) — Idler pulleys and end-stops

Build Idler Pulley Subassemblies from Socket Head Screws + Timing Belt Idlers + spacers, and attach them to the sides of the Low-Side U-Channel (4-stage kit: two subassemblies with 30mm screws/8mm spacers, plus six more with 16mm screws, for 8 idlers total; 2-stage kit: two + two, for 4 idlers total). Attach End-Stop Plates to the upper-stage Pulley Brackets with 5mm/16mm Button Head Screws and spacers, oriented in the "Active" configuration — the source guide includes reference figures showing correct Active vs. Inactive End-Stop Plate orientation at each position.

## Final steps — Motor and drive pulley

Fasten a 5203 Series Yellow Jacket Planetary Gear Motor (not included) to the Quad Block Mount using four 12mm Socket Head Screws. Fasten the 60-Tooth GT2 Timing Belt Pulley to the Sonic Hub using four 12mm Socket Head Screws, slide the Sonic Hub onto the motor's output shaft, and align the pulley with the timing belt idler/tensioner before tightening. **Tech tip from goBILDA:** a standard credit card (~1mm thick) is a handy spacer for setting the Sonic Hub's clearance from the U-Channel to get proper pulley alignment.

goBILDA's guide notes a separate rigging tutorial video for threading the belt through the assembled slide stages: `https://bit.ly/3J6Fn2q` (not fetched/mirrored here).
