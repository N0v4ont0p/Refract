# goBILDA Linear Actuator Kit — Assembly Guide

SKU: 3212-0001-0001. A lead-screw driven linear actuator (not a belt/chain slide) — a motor turns a gear pair that drives an 8mm lead screw through a captured nut, translating a goRAIL-mounted carriage plate along a 288mm U-Channel. Motor and motor mount are **not included**.

## Step 1 — Slide plate assembly

Attach the dual block mounts to the goRAIL slide plates using eight 8mm M4 screws.

## Step 2 — Mount into channel

Mount the slide plate assemblies into the U-Channel using eight 10mm M4 screws.

## Step 3 — Lead screw nut

Attach the lead screw nut to the end of the goRAIL using four 10mm M4 screws.

## Step 4 — Thread the lead screw

Thread the lead screw into the lead screw nut, then slide the goRAIL assembly into the slide plates. Put the collar and one thrust washer onto the lead screw, leaving the collar loose for now.

## Step 5 — Pillow block

Slide the pillow block onto the lead screw and fasten it to the channel using two 8mm M4 screws.

## Step 6 — Motor mount

Attach a clamping motor mount in place with two 10mm M4 screws. (Kit does not include a motor mount — goBILDA's guide shows a 1400-0032-0032 clamping mount as the reference example.)

## Step 7 — Motor

Insert a motor into the clamping mount and tighten the pinch bolt. (Kit does not include a motor — a 5202-0002-0005 Yellow Jacket is shown as the reference example.)

## Step 8 — Gear hubs

Mount the clamping hubs to the two 60-Tooth MOD 0.8 gears using eight 10mm M4 screws, noting gear orientation: the gear that attaches to the 8mm-bore hub should have its "dished out" side facing away from the hub.

## Step 9 — Final gear mesh

Position the gear assemblies on the motor shaft and lead screw. Slide the collar and gear together along the lead screw toward the pillow block. Tighten the pinch bolts on the collar and both hubs to set the gear mesh and lock the lead screw end float.
