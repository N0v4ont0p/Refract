# Cascading Low-Side U-Channel Kit — Assembly Guide

SKU: 3210-0001-0001. A cable/pulley-driven cascading linear slide built from Low-Side U-Channel segments and V-Guides — as one segment extends, "dolly" bearing carriages cascade the motion into the next segment, similar in concept to a cascade linear slide but built from U-channel rather than proprietary slide rail.

**Prerequisites:** this kit intentionally does **not** include a motor, motor mounts, Low-Side U-Channel, or V-Guides, so builders can size those to their own speed/stroke needs. The BOM below is goBILDA's own reference/buyer's-guide configuration used to write this guide.

## Steps 1–5 — Build a "long" segment (Segment A)

1. Sandwich a V-Groove Ball Bearing between two 0.25mm shims, slide it into a v-groove bearing shield, and insert a 25mm screw through the bores to make a pulley assembly.
2. Use a locknut and that pulley assembly to attach a Square Beam to the side of the longest (13-hole) Low-Side U-Channel. Tip: after tightening, you can adjust the Square Beam's angle with a hex key.
3. Attach a second Square Beam to the other side with a 16mm screw and locknut, keeping both beams parallel to the channel.
4. Slide a Slide Guide Plate onto the channel and fasten it to the Square Beams with two 12mm screws.
5. Stand the assembly up with the plastic pulley shield hanging off the work surface edge; stand a V-Guide alongside it (ends flush) and fasten the V-Guide to the channel with twelve 8mm screws.

## Step 6 — Build two "short" segments (Segments B & C)

Repeat Steps 1–5 using two of the three remaining shorter (11-hole) Low-Side U-Channels.

## Steps 7–9 — Build the pulley end-cap segment (Segment D)

Using the last Low-Side U-Channel, build a pulley end-cap assembly: four 20mm screws (two through the bottom pulley endcap plate + channel, two through just the plastic plate), each with a 0.25mm shim stacked on. Stack two 6mm spacers and two V-Groove Ball Bearings, each with a 0.25mm shim. Fit the top pulley endcap plate and fasten with four locknuts.

You now have four labeled segments: **A** (long), **B**, **C**, **D** (short).

## Step 10 — Dolly assemblies

Fasten two V-Groove Ball Bearings to a flat mount using two 10mm screws, with a 0.25mm shim under each bearing so it spins freely. Repeat for a total of six dolly assemblies. Tip: loosen the two screws and squeeze the bearings together before re-tightening for a tighter fit on the V-Guide.

## Steps 11–13 — Cascade the segments together

11. Slide two dolly assemblies onto Segment B's V-Guide, facing away from each other (butterfly shape), positioned about halfway down. Slide Segment A into Segment B's Slide Guide Plate, and fasten Segment A to the dollies with four 6mm screws.
12. Install two more dolly assemblies on Segment C; slide Segment B through Segment C's Slide Guide Plate and fasten with four 6mm screws.
13. Repeat with the last two dolly assemblies to join Segment C to Segment D.

## Steps 14–15 — Cable-routing pulleys

Build three more pulley assemblies as in Step 1 (25mm screw, V-Groove Ball Bearing, bearing shield, two 0.25mm shims), this time adding an 8mm spacer on the end. Mount these three pulley assemblies (one locknut each) on the last hole of the Low-Side U-Channel on Segments B, C, and D.

## Step 16 — Motor and winch

Mount two clamping mounts using four 25mm screws and four 16mm spacers. Slide the motor into the clamping mounts and tighten the pinch bolts. Fasten the winch pulley to the Sonic Hub with four 8mm screws, then slip the hub onto the motor shaft and tighten the pinch bolts.

Assembly complete. goBILDA notes to check their separate Rigging Guide document next for cable-routing the cascade (not fetched/mirrored here).
