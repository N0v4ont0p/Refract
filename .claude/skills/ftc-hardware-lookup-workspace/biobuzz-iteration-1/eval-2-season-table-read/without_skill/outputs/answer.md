**Yes. The WATTOS Stingray 12V DC motor (part number WDM12) is legal for the 2026-2027 season (BIOBUZZ).**

I checked the official Competition Manual, V1, downloaded from ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual. The motor is in **Section 12.5, Rule R501, Table 12-1 ("Motor allowances")**, listed as "WATTOS Stingray 12V DC, WDM12". R501 says only the motors in that table are allowed.

What to watch at inspection:
- **Match the part number.** The motor on the robot should be the WDM12. Other WATTOS motors are not on the list. The table notes that you can run a listed gearmotor with or without its gearbox, or with any other compatible gearbox.
- **8-motor limit (R503).** The robot can have at most 8 motors and 8 servos in total, counting every mechanism in every configuration you use at an event. Each Stingray counts toward that 8.
- **No changes to the motor (R504).** You can only change the mounting brackets or the output shaft (pinions included), and trim the wires or add connectors per R609. The motor's internal mechanical and electrical parts must stay stock.
- **Check for newer versions.** I read V1 only and did not check Team Updates or later manual versions. They could change the table, though the manual says motors "may be added" in future updates and doesn't mention removals. Before inspection, confirm on the FIRST Game & Season page that V1 is still the latest.

Other WATTOS parts on the list: the "WATTOS 12V Battery" (WT-NMH1230) and the "WATTOS Power Switch Kit" (WTS-SW1220) are also in the manual's approved lists. If you plan to use those too, check the full battery and power-switch rules as well.

Questions I would ask if I could:
1. Is the motor you have actually the WDM12? If not, the answer is no, because only the WDM12 is listed.
2. How many motors and servos does the robot have in all configurations? If adding Stingrays takes you over 8 motors, it breaks R503.
3. Have you changed the motor beyond trimming wires or swapping the pinion or bracket? If so, check it against R504 before inspection.
