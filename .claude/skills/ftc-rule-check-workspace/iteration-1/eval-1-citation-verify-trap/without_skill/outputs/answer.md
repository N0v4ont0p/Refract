Short answer: yes, 19 inches wide is very likely a problem.

In FTC, the robot has to fit inside an **18 x 18 x 18 inch** volume in its starting configuration — the size it is sitting on the field right before the match begins (before the match starts and it's allowed to expand). At 19 inches wide, you're an inch over on one dimension, so an inspector would fail you at sizing inspection until you get it under 18 inches.

A couple of important caveats:

- **"About 19 inches" isn't precise enough.** Inspectors use a physical sizing tool/box, and it's checked with the robot in its true starting state. You need to measure carefully with calipers or a tape against a hard edge, including anything that sticks out (bumpers, wheels, brackets, zip-tie tails, wiring). If you're reading 19", you are over — there's no rounding in your favor.

- **It's the *starting* size that's constrained.** Once the match starts, the robot is allowed to expand beyond the 18" cube (up to a larger expansion limit that has varied by season). So a 19" resting width is only a problem if that's the pre-match/starting footprint — which is exactly what you described.

- **The exact size number can change between seasons and via Team Updates.** 18x18x18 has been the standard starting envelope for many recent seasons, but you should confirm the current season's number, because it occasionally changes.

What I'd do: shave that dimension down below 18 inches (retract or fold whatever is sticking out into the starting configuration), then re-measure with the robot fully in its start state. Aim for a little margin (say 17.5") so you're not fighting the sizing box on the edge.

Honesty note on the rule citation: I'm answering from general knowledge of how FTC sizing works, not from the current manual in front of me. The relevant rule is the robot-sizing rule in the "Robot Construction Rules" section of the Competition Manual (in recent seasons this has been rule R104, sometimes referenced alongside the expansion-limit rule). I'm not fully certain of the exact rule number or the exact current dimension for this season, so before you rely on it for inspection, please verify the current sizing rule and its number in this season's official Game Manual Part 1 (and any Team Updates). I don't want to hand you a rule number I can't confirm.
