### What the manual says

**1. Scoring rules for FLOWERS (Section 10.5.2).** This is part of the manual itself, not a numbered rule. Quoted exactly:
> "Placing SCORING ELEMENTS into the top of the FLOWER is the only allowable way to score. ROBOTS must follow G418 while interacting with the FLOWER."

The glossary definition of FLOWER uses the same wording: "a structure on the FIELD in which POLLEN and NECTAR can be placed into the top, and POLLEN can be removed from the bottom".

**2. LAUNCHING is a defined term, and the manual uses it only for the HIVE.** Glossary, quoted exactly:
> "an action by a ROBOT in which the SCORING ELEMENT is shot into the air, propelled across the floor to a desired location or in a preferred direction, or thrown in a forceful way"

Section 10.5.1 (HIVE scoring) says: "LAUNCHING into the upward-facing CELL is the only allowed way to earn a HIVE TIP." The game overview says the same split: POLLEN is placed into FLOWERS and LAUNCHED into HIVE CELLS. Using "only placing" for one goal and "only LAUNCHING" for the other looks deliberate. A flywheel shot from a distance is clearly LAUNCHING under that definition, so it isn't placing.

**3. The penalty rule for FLOWERS (G418) limits where POLLEN goes in, not how it gets there.** POLLEN may only enter through the top. Anything else meant to score is a violation. A shot that goes in the top doesn't clearly break G418. It still falls outside Section 10.5.2's "only allowable way to score."

### Why the verdict is "ambiguous"
- **"Place" is not a defined term.** I looked up PLACE, PLACED and SCORED in the glossary and none of them exist. LAUNCH is defined; placing isn't. A slow drop from an arm or chute held over the opening is probably placing. A flywheel shot from across the field is clearly LAUNCHING. Anything in between is for the referees to call, and there's no text to settle it.
- **I found no rule with a penalty aimed specifically at launching into a FLOWER.** The rules I checked were G405 through G418 and the FLOWER and HIVE scoring sections. The risk is mainly that the POLLEN doesn't score, plus the side effects below. It isn't a clear named foul.
- **Whether a POLLEN counts as "in" also depends on a CAD drawing.** Section 10.5.2 defines the scoring volume as "between the top ring and the middle ring… included in CAD Reference 10-4". The rules database only stores text, not drawings, so it can't tell you exactly where that volume ends.

### Other rules a launcher would run into
- **G405:** "A ROBOT may not deliberately eject a SCORING ELEMENT from the FIELD… Violation: MAJOR FOUL per SCORING ELEMENT." POLLEN that leaves the field during a real scoring attempt is exempt. Whether a FLOWER shot counts as a real scoring attempt is exactly the open question above.
- **G418:** You get a VERBAL WARNING. It becomes a MAJOR FOUL plus a YELLOW CARD if the referees judge it STRATEGIC (deliberate). POLLEN you shoot at the FLOWER that knocks already-scored POLLEN out, or hits the side, gets judged under G418.
- **G407:** Once a LAUNCHED POLLEN has left your robot, it no longer counts toward your 4-element limit. That part helps a launcher.
