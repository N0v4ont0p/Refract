**No, not deliberately.** Pushing a pile of 6 POLLEN across the field on purpose counts as CONTROLLING all 6, and that breaks the 4-element limit. I checked the V1 2026-27 Competition Manual PDF (downloaded from ftc-resources.firstinspires.org).

**Why pushing counts as CONTROL (Glossary, "CONTROL")**
- CONTROL isn't only picking things up. It also covers a robot that "intentionally pushes a SCORING ELEMENT to a desired location or in a preferred direction (i.e., herding)."
- The contact can be passed through other pieces ("transitively through other SCORING ELEMENTS"). So the balls at the back of the pile that aren't touching your robot still count.
- One sign of CONTROL the refs use is the robot "moving the SCORING ELEMENT in a preferred direction with a flat or concave face of the ROBOT." The front of a drivetrain is exactly that.

**The rule you'd break: G407, "No more than 4 at a time"**
- A robot may not CONTROL more than 4 SCORING ELEMENTS at once.
- The penalty is a VERBAL WARNING, or a MAJOR FOUL plus a YELLOW CARD per match if it's STRATEGIC (done to gain an advantage).
- The rule says robots controlling 5 or more "will be under scrutiny." It lists "multiple instances of greater than MOMENTARY CONTROL of 5 or more" as likely STRATEGIC. MOMENTARY means under about 3 seconds.
- Driving 6 balls across the field takes longer than 3 seconds and is clearly on purpose. Expect a MAJOR FOUL and a YELLOW CARD, not just a warning.

**What "bulldozing" means here**
G407 does say bulldozing is not CONTROL, but only in a narrow sense: "inadvertent contact with a SCORING ELEMENT while in the path of the ROBOT moving about the FIELD." If you drive somewhere and happen to shove a ball or two, that's fine. Deliberately driving a pile to a spot is herding, even if it looks the same.

**Other rules to watch**
- **G411 (no hoarding):** Corralling pieces into a small part of the field, or blocking the other alliance from reaching them, is a MAJOR FOUL plus a YELLOW CARD. Pushing your own POLLEN to where you'll score it is fine, but pushing a big pile into a corner can also trigger this.
- **G418 (FLOWERS):** POLLEN may only go in through the top of a FLOWER. You can't push balls into one from the side or bottom.
- **G405:** Don't push balls off the field on purpose. That's a MAJOR FOUL per ball.

**What you can legally do**
- Herd up to 4 POLLEN at a time with the front of the robot.
- Or push a small group, then back off so at least one ball is left behind, before you go over 4 for more than a moment.
- Consider adding a guard or a narrow front so the robot can't gather a big pile by accident.

This is from V1 of the manual. Team Updates and the official Q&A can change or clarify G407 and the CONTROL definition, so check them before your first event.

**Questions I'd ask you (no one was available to answer):**
