**What the green asterisk means.** Rules with a green headline and a leading asterisk are "evergreen" rules. FIRST says the rule's intent and presence stay roughly the same each season, but the wording can be updated. It marks the rule as a whole. It does not promise that the specific parts, part numbers or referenced tables inside the rule are unchanged. Two other catches:
- Rule numbers can be reassigned between manuals. Don't assume last year's R501 and this year's R501 are the same rule.
- We're now in the 2026-2027 BIOBUZZ season. DECODE was 2025-2026, and its manual went through Team Update 32. A DECODE thread describes the legal list back then. It says nothing about V1 of the BIOBUZZ manual or any Team Updates since.

**About the WATTOS Stingray.** I couldn't confirm that a "WATTOS Stingray" motor exists or is legal. WATTOS shows up on Chief Delphi as a newer vendor making FRC/FTC parts, mainly for the Turkey region. I found no Stingray product page and nothing putting it on an FTC legal-motor list, and I won't guess its specs or legality from memory. Also, a motor from a smaller vendor being legal usually depends on exact manufacturer and part-number wording. Close enough won't pass at inspection.

**How to settle it:**
1. Open the current 2026-2027 Competition Manual. Find the rule that lists allowed DC motors by its text, not by the number R501.
2. Check whether the Stingray's exact manufacturer and part number is listed, and apply any other limits the rule sets, such as motor count.
3. Check every Team Update released since V1.
4. If it's still unclear, ask on the official FTC Q&A system. The answer counts when it's posted this season. A DECODE-era answer doesn't.
