Yes, the WATTOS Stingray is legal. But the rule is R501, not R510. "R510" in Team Update 00 is a typo.

I checked both official documents posted September 12, 2026: the V1 2026-2027 BIOBUZZ Competition Manual and Team Update 00.

**What each document says**
- **Team Update 00 (page 3):** it lists "R510 *Only specific motors are allowed" and says the WATTOS Stingray has been added to the allowed motors list.
- **The V1 manual:** there is no rule R510 anywhere in it. The rule with that exact title is **R501**, in Section 12.5 Motors & Actuators. Its Table 12-1 includes the row **"WATTOS Stingray 12V DC — WDM12"**.
- **Which one wins:** the manual is the official rule text, and Team Updates describe changes to it. Team Update 00 also says it doesn't cover rule-number or reference changes. So cite **R501** when talking to an inspector.

**Rules that still apply to it**
- **R503:** your robot can have at most 8 motors in total, counting all configurations. The Stingray counts toward that limit like any other allowed motor. Only built-in phone/computer motors and motors inside a sensor are exempt.
- **R504:** you can't modify the motor. You may change the mounting, output shaft or pinion, trim the leads and add connectors, add labels, and do repairs or maintenance the manufacturer recommends.
- **R505:** it must be controlled and powered through an approved motor controller.
- The manual also says gearmotors can be used with or without the gearbox they came with, or with any compatible gearbox.

**Things to check**
1. **Is your motor part number WDM12?** If yes, it's legal as shown above. If it's a different WATTOS part, it isn't on the list, so it's not legal unless a later Team Update adds it.
2. **Do you already have 8 motors?** If adding the Stingray takes you past 8, you'd have to remove another motor to stay within R503.
3. **Does an inspector question the rule number?** Show them R501 and Table 12-1 in the V1 manual. If you want an official correction of the typo, the Q&A system opens September 28, 2026, at 12:00 p.m. ET.
