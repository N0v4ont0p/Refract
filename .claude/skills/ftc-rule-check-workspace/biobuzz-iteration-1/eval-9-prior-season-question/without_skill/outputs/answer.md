**G416 in DECODE (2025-2026) was the rule that you could only launch from inside a launch zone.** I checked this against the final archived manual (Section 11, V15, the Team Update 32 version).

**The rule:** "LAUNCHING in the LAUNCH ZONE only." A robot may only launch scoring elements while it is inside a LAUNCH ZONE or overlapping a LAUNCH LINE.

**Penalty:**
- A MINOR FOUL for each element launched from outside a launch zone.
- A MAJOR FOUL for each one if that element goes into the open top of the GOAL.

So shooting from the wrong spot and scoring cost you more than shooting and missing.

**Key definitions:**
- **LAUNCH ZONE:** there were two triangular zones bounded by the white LAUNCH LINE tape and the field wall, and they extend infinitely upward. The small one was on the audience side (2 tiles wide by 1 tile deep). The big one was on the goal side (6 tiles wide by 3 tiles deep). The tape counts as part of the zone, so a robot just touching the line was legal.
- **LAUNCHED:** an element counts as launched if it's shot into the air, pushed across the floor toward a chosen spot or direction, or thrown forcefully.

**What the rule did not penalize:**
- **Bulldozing:** a robot driving around and bumping into elements that happen to be in its path.
- **Normal manipulator use:**
  - Running an intake in reverse so an element rolls a short distance away.
  - Pushing an element a short distance while herding it across the field.

**How to settle the argument:**
- **If the dispute was about shooting from mid-field or while crossing between zones:** that was a G416 foul. Being partly over the line was enough to make it legal.
- **If it was about spitting balls out or pushing them around:** G416 allowed that, unless it was forceful enough to count as a throw or a floor shot aimed at a target. Whether it was went to the referee.
- **If someone remembers G416 as a different rule:** the number could be different in an earlier version of the manual. The rule about touching or closing GATES is G417, and the rule about touching ARTIFACTS on RAMPS is G418. It's worth checking which manual version your match used.
