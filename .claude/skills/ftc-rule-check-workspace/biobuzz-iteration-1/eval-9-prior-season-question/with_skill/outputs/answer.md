**In DECODE (2025-26), G416 limited where a robot could launch game pieces: only from inside a LAUNCH ZONE or while touching a LAUNCH LINE.**

I checked this against the DECODE rules corpus, which includes changes through Team Update 32. The rule was never changed by a Team Update; it is the same as in the original manual.

**Rule text (G416, "LAUNCHING in the LAUNCH ZONE only", section 11.4.4 ROBOT), exactly as stored:**
> "ROBOTS may only LAUNCH SCORING ELEMENTS when inside a LAUNCH ZONE or overlapping a LAUNCH LINE. Violation: MINOR FOUL per LAUNCHED SCORING ELEMENT. MAJOR FOUL per LAUNCHED SCORING ELEMENT if the SCORING ELEMENT enters the open top of the GOAL. A SCORING ELEMENT is considered LAUNCHED if it is shot into the air, propelled across the floor to a desired location or in a preferred direction, or thrown in a forceful way. "Bulldozing" (inadvertent contact with a SCORING ELEMENT while in the path of the ROBOT moving about the FIELD) is not considered LAUNCHING This is not intended to penalize teams with active manipulators which are expelling SCORING ELEMENTS through normal operation, such as: A. Running an intake in reverse causing a SCORING ELEMENT to travel a short distance from the ROBOT. B. A ROBOT pushing a SCORING ELEMENT a short distance away in the process of herding it across the FIELD."

**What that means in practice:**
1. **Where you could launch:** only from inside a LAUNCH ZONE or overlapping a LAUNCH LINE.
   - The glossary defines a LAUNCH ZONE as "infinitely tall triangular volumes bounded by LAUNCH LINES and the FIELD perimeter".
   - A LAUNCH LINE is "the white tape which bounds 2 triangular LAUNCH ZONES, as well as 2 segments of white tape located at the base of the GOAL".
2. **What counted as launching:** the rule defines it itself ("LAUNCH" is not in the glossary). Any of these counted:
   - shooting a piece into the air
   - pushing it across the floor toward a chosen spot or in a chosen direction
   - throwing it forcefully
3. **What did not count:**
   - Bulldozing: accidentally hitting a piece that is in your path while driving.
   - Normal manipulator action: running an intake in reverse so a piece travels a short distance, or pushing a piece a short way while herding it.
4. **Penalty:** it was charged per piece launched from outside the allowed area.
   - Normally a MINOR FOUL for each piece.
   - A MAJOR FOUL for each piece that went into the open top of the GOAL.
