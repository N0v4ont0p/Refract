**It's on your side.** The red GARDEN is in a corner next to the red ALLIANCE AREA, not across the field. The blue GARDEN is in the corner diagonally opposite, next to the blue ALLIANCE AREA. I checked this in the official 2026-27 BIOBUZZ Competition Manual (V1), downloaded from ftc-resources.firstinspires.org.

**What the manual says:**
- **Section 9.3:** the GARDEN is a strip about 23 in. by 2 in., marked by red or blue tape "in opposite corners of the FIELD" (Figure 9-2).
- **Figure 9-2 (page 65):** red ALLIANCE AREA is on the left wall. The red GARDEN is in the bottom-left corner, running along the bottom wall. The red LOADING ZONE is at the other end of the red wall (upper-left). Blue is the mirror image: blue GARDEN top-right along the top wall, blue LOADING ZONE lower-right.
- **Figure 9-3 (page 66):** the GARDEN tape runs along the wall that is perpendicular to your alliance wall, starting right in the corner. It is not on the alliance wall itself.
- **Section 10.3.1:** the 4 pre-staged POLLEN in each GARDEN sit in a line "starting in the corner closest to the ALLIANCE AREA," touching the audience or rear wall.

**For your auto paths:**
- Red GARDEN and red LOADING ZONE are at opposite ends of your own wall. Parking (the LOADING ZONE counts for PARK points) and scoring in the GARDEN pull toward different corners.
- Your GARDEN starts with 4 POLLEN already in it, and the other alliance can remove them. GARDENs are not protected zones (10.5.3).
- The blue GARDEN is the far diagonal corner. POLLEN in either GARDEN scores for that GARDEN's color, no matter who put it there.

**One thing to confirm:** figures are shown from a fixed view, and the text only says the GARDEN touches the "audience or rear" wall. Before you hard-code which of your two corners it's in, check the Event Field Setup Guide or look at a real field. Also check for any Team Update newer than V1 that moves field elements.

Questions I'd ask if someone were available:
1. **Which red starting spot are you planning for, near the GARDEN corner or near the LOADING ZONE end?** That decides whether auto goes score-then-park or the reverse, and whether the path runs along the wall or diagonally.
2. **Do you mirror your blue paths from red, or write them separately?** Blue is diagonally symmetric (turned 180 degrees), not a left-right flip. A mirror across one axis would put your blue GARDEN in the wrong corner.
