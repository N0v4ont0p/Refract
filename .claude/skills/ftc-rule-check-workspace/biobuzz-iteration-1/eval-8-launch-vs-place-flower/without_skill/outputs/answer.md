**Short answer: no, plan on placing it.** V1 of the BIOBUZZ Competition Manual (released at kickoff on Sept 12, 2026) says in the section 10.5.2 FLOWER Scoring Criteria note that "Placing SCORING ELEMENTS into the top of the FLOWER is the only allowable way to score." No rule specifically bans launching at a FLOWER. But a shot ball is very unlikely to be counted, and it can get you into trouble with G418.

**What the V1 manual says**
- **10.5.2 FLOWER Scoring Criteria.** POLLEN and NECTAR score when at least partly inside the scoring volume, between the top ring and the middle ring. The note right under it is the "placing is the only allowable way" line quoted above. It also says robots must follow G418 around FLOWERS.
- **Launching is only mentioned for the HIVE.** Section 10.5.1 says launching into the upward-facing CELL is the only allowed way to tip a HIVE. Section 10.1 describes robots "place into the FLOWERS" and "LAUNCH them into their CELLS." The glossary defines a FLOWER as a structure where scoring elements "can be placed into the top."
- **Definition of LAUNCH (glossary).** A scoring element that is shot into the air, rolled across the floor on purpose, or thrown forcefully. A flywheel shooter clearly counts.
- **G418 (don't meddle with FLOWERS).** Robots may only put POLLEN and NECTAR in through the top and only take POLLEN out through the bottom. The penalty is a verbal warning, or a MAJOR FOUL plus a YELLOW CARD per match if the refs think it was deliberate. G418 only controls which way the ball goes in, so a shot that drops through the top doesn't clearly break it. That gap is why this is "not allowed to score" rather than a named foul.

**Why it isn't worth it even if a ref let it slide**
- **The target is small.** The top opening is about 4 in. across, about 21.5 in. off the tiles, with a backstop only 1.25 in. tall. POLLEN is about 3 in. across, so launched balls will bounce off. Balls you knock loose can then land you in G418 trouble.
- **The points go to whoever owns the FLOWER.** POLLEN in a FLOWER is worth 2 points each in TELEOP, and only for the alliance whose NECTAR is top-most. If you don't own that FLOWER, your POLLEN scores for the other alliance.
- **Timing.** The manual says FLOWER scoring starts at 1:00 remaining. Scoring earlier still counts but can draw penalties. G410 gives a MAJOR FOUL per NECTAR entered early.
- **Your launcher's real job is the HIVE.** Each HIVE TIP is worth 20 points in AUTO or TELEOP, POLLEN left in a CELL is worth 2, and the POLLINATOR ranking points come from tips. FLOWERS need a short place-from-above mechanism: a slow low-speed release, a chute, or a drop from a pivoting arm.
